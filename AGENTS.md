# AGENTS.md

## Working agreement

This file governs work in `JamesTRichmond/middlebury-campus-rpg`. Deliver the goal in [GOAL.md](GOAL.md) by executing [LOOP.md](LOOP.md). These are boundaries, not an implementation recipe. Choose the simplest sound approach inside them.

Follow the host's instruction hierarchy. This document does not grant tools, credentials, network access, or permission to bypass approval controls. Do not assume memory of previous chats. The repo is the memory: STATUS, BACKLOG, DECISIONS, and `cycles/`.

A host goal that says "build the RPG" means "run the loop." It does not mean "implement every phase in one diff."

## Authority

1. Routine reversible work inside the current backlog item is pre-authorized. Asking "want me to continue?" between cycles is a failure. Asking is required only for a human gate or a choice that changes privacy, cost, affiliation, or an external system.
2. Do not disable a feature, swallow an error, or substitute a mock to make a gate pass. Fix the cause.
3. Do not weaken tests, schemas, or the content policy to get a green run.
4. Do not do drive-by refactors, dependency upgrades, or redesigns outside the current item.
5. Preserve other agents' work. No force-push, no history rewrite, no deleting cycle reports.
6. Do not edit this file, GOAL.md, or LOOP.md to permit something they forbid. A change to the working rules is its own backlog item and must be reviewable.
7. Direct pushes to the default branch are allowed only when the host environment permits them and you are the integrator. Otherwise use `cycle/<id>` and a pull request, and do not merge it yourself.
8. Do not deploy, buy a domain, turn on a paid API, or connect a college system without a human gate file in `gates/`. The Figma integration already connected for this repo is authorized. Use it without asking. If the call fails, that is a human gate.

## Product invariants

1. **Unofficial.** The game is not Middlebury College. Do not imply endorsement. Do not copy logos, crests, or athletic marks. Map sprites and portraits are original art. Cozy-game references are for feel only. Do not copy another game's characters, tiles, or UI.
2. **No invented people.** NPCs are archetypes until a gate says otherwise. A real name, biography, quotation, headshot, or "this drawing is that person" needs `gates/likeness.approved` naming them. `gates/real-people.approved` is the older biography gate; either file must contain `approved-by:`. Absence means original art and a role title, not President Baucom and not a scraped staff photo.
3. **Facts have a status.** UI that states a campus fact must show it as verified, seed, or sample. Only `verified` facts may be phrased as currently true, and they require `sourceUrl` and `asOf`. The Triangle Park campus-boundary reading is fiction; label it. See [docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md](docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md).
4. **Lessons are data.** Classrooms call `LessonSource`. Bundled lessons are samples. A lesson must not name a real instructor or claim to be an official course section. Live LMS wiring waits on `gates/live-lms.approved`.
5. **The save is the player's.** `localStorage` key prefix `midd-campus-rpg.save`. Version it. Do not drop notes, quests, room doodads, accessibility settings, or unlocks on load. No accounts and no server database in v1.
6. **The graph is the logic, the pixel map is the view.** Places, exits, NPCs, quests, and abilities are JSON under `content/`, checked by `npm run validate`. The player walks a top-down pixel map of that graph (D009). Do not keep a second set of doors in the UI, and do not ship exit-buttons as the final travel UI.
7. **Progression is arrival, not punishment.** Tasks stay open if skipped. The wheelchair route is part of correctness for the escort. Abilities unlock actions.
8. **Figma first.** Player-visible design lives in the connected Figma file. [docs/FIGMA.md](docs/FIGMA.md) is the setup. Do not invent a Figma URL. A failed Figma call is a human gate, not a reason to skip the file.
